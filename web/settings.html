KVM serial

The baud rate is saved on the controller. With "Add checksum byte" ticked, the KVM's checksum (the low byte of the sum of all bytes) is added to the end. For example, AA BB 03 01 01 becomes AA BB 03 01 01 6A. Replies, and anything the KVM sends by itself, show up in the log. The status query is from the manual, and this KVM hasn't answered it yet.

Backup & reset


Export settings



  
  





The settings file holds the controller name, PC names, presets, Companion settings and baud rate. Importing one replaces all of them. Factory reset erases every saved setting and restarts the controller. Neither one touches the Wi-Fi details, API token or web login, which are built into the firmware.







Firmware update



  
  




Upload .pio/build/esp32dev/firmware.bin. The controller restarts once it's installed.
